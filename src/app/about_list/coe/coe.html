Controller of Examination

Mr. N.P. Singh

COE, Starex University

Mr. N.P. Singh assumed the charge of Controller of Examination of Starex University, Gurugram on 1st August, 2023. Prior to joining, he served as Controller of Examination at Shri Lal Bahadur Shastri National Sanskrit University, New Delhi.

He held key roles at IGNOU, including Registrar (Student Evaluation), and led initiatives in Community College and Defense Units. With over 35 years of experience, Mr. Singh has contributed to Administration, Finance, Student Services, Vigilance, and Examination Management.

His expertise spans entrance test coordination, large-scale exam execution, and answer script evaluation. He has also overseen Regional and Study Centers across India.

Mr. Singh has served on multiple university committees including Board of Management, Finance, Academic Council, and Promotion Rules framing bodies.
