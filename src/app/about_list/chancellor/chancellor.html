We expect that during your stay in the University, you will emerge as real human beings with the ability and capability of innovation, creation and decision-making zeal for ushering in a strong and lucrative career. Now, let me introduce you to our Vision and Mission.

Vision

'Nunc Est Semper: Now is Forever' is our motto which indicates our vision to spread the light of education among the scholars and the masses forever and to contribute towards their growth and empowerment.

To develop into a center of academic excellence and promote Professional, Technical & Vocational expertise and research.

To instill in our students a deep awareness of today’s global challenges and equip them to thrive on professionally and technically driven platforms.

Mission

To impart quality education to students.

To create a conducive, multi-disciplinary, vibrant and inspirational environment for academic growth and overall development.

To foster academic excellence and develop elegant communication skills to meet global demands.

To contribute new perspectives to world knowledge.

To develop critical thinkers and responsible citizens.

To nurture lifelong learners with a hunger for knowledge.
