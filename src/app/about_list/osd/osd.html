OSD Message

Dr. A.S. Bedi, a seasoned professional with an illustrious career spanning several decades in diverse industries. With a rich and varied background, Dr. A.S. Bedi brings a wealth of experience and expertise to our organization.

Having commenced his journey in an International Company, Taylor Instrument India Ltd., in collaboration with IBM Inc. U.S.A, under the visionary leadership of Sh. C.K. Birla. Dr. A.S. Bedi served diligently in various capacities for an impressive duration of 20 years. Throughout this tenure, he excelled as Purchase & Stores Manager, Sales Manager, E.D.P. Manager, and ultimately, as General Manager, leaving an indelible mark on the company's growth and success.

Subsequently, Dr. A.S. Bedi continued to thrive in the corporate world, holding the position of General Manager at Chip Computers, a subsidiary of International Export Co. (M/s Vikram Overseas Pvt. Ltd.) for three fruitful years. This was followed by another remarkable stint as General Manager (Marketing) at M/s Graphic Systems Ltd., a highly reputed National company, where he demonstrated exceptional marketing acumen and strategic vision, contributing significantly to the organization's expansion and recognition.

In addition to his outstanding corporate achievements, Dr. A.S. Bedi has also showcased his proficiency in the education sector. He served as the Chief Administrator of Shemrock Jagat Convent School, Paschim Vihar, Delhi, ensuring the smooth and efficient functioning of the institution for three years. Furthermore, his commitment to education led him to hold the esteemed position of Vice President at two prestigious schools in Delhi for an impressive six-year period.

Beyond his professional accomplishments, Dr. A.S. Bedi is widely regarded for his extensive social connections with esteemed personalities across India. His ability to foster meaningful relationships with influential figures has been instrumental in forging strong partnerships and collaborations for various endeavors.

As OSD cum Chairman's Secretary, Dr. A.S. Bedi brings a unique blend of corporate acumen, educational expertise, and social finesse, making them an invaluable asset to our organization. With his proven track record of excellence, dedication, and versatility, we are confident that he will play a pivotal role in supporting our Chairman's vision and contributing to the continued growth and success of our organization.

Dr. A.S. Bedi (D.Phil. in English Literature)

OSD cum Secretary to Chairman, Starex Foundation.
