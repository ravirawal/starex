Functions of Corporate Resource Center

We are committed to making our students industry-ready through theoretical & practical exposure and campus placements.

The Corporate Resource Center (CRC) at Starex University is dedicated to bridging the gap between education and employment. In today’s competitive landscape, CRC not only facilitates placements but also empowers students for self-employment and marketing roles through communication skill development in Language Labs.

Our dynamic training & placement team prepares students through guest lectures, career counselling, internships, and live projects—ensuring they become top choices for leading corporates and MNCs like Wipro and Byju’s.

Key Functions

  • Manage campus training and placement activities
  • Establish strong industry connections for placements
  • Guide students on public sector careers (SSC, UPSC, PSU, Banking)
  • Collect feedback from recruiting companies
  • Conduct soft skills development programs
  • Organize interview preparation sessions with experts
  • Host entrepreneurship workshops for self-employment readiness
  • Introduce students to Skill India and Startup India schemes
