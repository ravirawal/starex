Functions of Corporate Resource Center

We are committed to making our students industry-ready through theoretical & practical exposure and campus placements.

The jobs for educated youth are available in the public and private sectors but are not sufficient. The Corporate Resource Center (CRC) at Starex University is committed to creating job opportunities in the present times of unemployment. The forte of the Corporate Resource Center, Starex University is to encourage them for self-employment also. Not only this, but we also are committed to delivering communication skills in Language Labs to create suitable manpower for marketing as maximum jobs available in marketing. Our dynamic training & placement team makes the students industry-ready through various guest lectures, training sessions for the placements, career counselling, internships, live projects, etc. This imbibes all the required skills among the students to let them show their calibre and expertise in the campus placement drives and be the top priority of the leading corporates and top MNCs in India like Wipro, Byjus, etc.

Functions of Corporate Resource Center

Providing the Excellent Career Support to Let You Begin Your Journey

  • To look after the training and placement activities of students on the campus.
  • To have close liaising with industries for the placement of students.
  • To make students aware of public sector jobs, including SSC (Staff Selection Commission), UPSC (Union Public Service Commission) and another state Public Service Commission, PSU's (Public Sector Undertakings), and banking jobs, and corresponding skills required.
  • To collect feedback from the companies coming for placement.
  • To arrange training programmes for the development of soft skills.
  • To arrange training programmes for students to face interviews using institutional and external expertise.
  • To organize entrepreneurship workshops to make the students ready for self-employment.
  • To get the students acquainted with Skill India Schemes including Startup India Scheme.
