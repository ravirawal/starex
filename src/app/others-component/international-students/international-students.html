Required Documents (UGC Guidelines)

  • Valid student visa
  • Original passport with visa endorsement

Local Guardian Policy

A local guardian may be a close family member or trusted friend residing in India. They must:

  • Be over 21 years of age
  • Have good moral character
  • Be an Indian citizen or hold a valid visa
