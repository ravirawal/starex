IBM (ICE) Program Highlights

IBM & Starex University Collaboration

Starex University has partnered with IBM Innovation Centre for Education (IBM-ICE) since 2021–22 to deliver cutting-edge programs in emerging technologies. This collaboration is a pioneering step in the Delhi-NCR region, offering applied IT knowledge through IBM’s Industry-Academia Collaboration Framework.

Students benefit from advanced applied learning, industry interactions, and future-ready training that bridges academic excellence with real-world relevance.

About IBM ICE Programs

Traditional computer science programs are evolving. IBM ICE programs at Starex University offer applied, industry-oriented training designed to meet the demands of modern employers. Students gain hands-on experience, global content, and exposure to advanced techniques through:

  • Project-based and lab-driven modules
  • Industry-focused curriculum with expert interactions
  • Global and inter-college competitions for holistic development

Benefits of IBM ICE Programs

Exclusive Courseware

Custom content developed by IBM experts, updated regularly for relevance and concurrency.

Cutting-Edge Training

Access to IBM’s global e-learning ecosystem, including internships and project-based learning.

Lab-Based Learning

Applied modules with real-world projects to reinforce theoretical concepts.

Expert Interaction

Scheduled visits and mentorship from IBM professionals to inspire and guide students.

Global Competitions

Participation in hackathons, poster presentations, and contests at national and international levels.
