The Apprenticeship Training Program for Hospitality, Germany, Europe

Starex University invites candidates aged 18–29 with BSL to embark on a transformative LEARN, EARN, WORK model.

This program offers a paid apprenticeship with monthly stipends of €640–950 at top properties in Germany, while pursuing a 3-year vocational degree sponsored by the Federal State Government.

Training alternates between two weeks of hands-on hotel/restaurant experience and two weeks of theoretical instruction under the German Government Training Institute.

Graduates earn specialist degrees as cooks, restaurant specialists, hotel specialists, or system gastronomy professionals—with guaranteed employment.

The degree is recognized across Germany, Switzerland, and the EU, offering a pathway to Permanent Residency within 5 years.

This program empowers candidates to become financially independent while advancing their careers in one of the world’s most progressive economies.
