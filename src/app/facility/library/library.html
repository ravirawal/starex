📚 Mahatma Budh Central Library

The Mahatma Budh Central Library is the sanctum sanctorum of Starex University. Designed by a renowned German architect, it stands as a beacon of knowledge for students, scholars, faculty, and staff.

Equipped with modern facilities and KOHA automation software, the library offers access to academic resources including books, journals, DVDs, and digital repositories like DELNET, Manupatra, West Law, ELSEVIER, and the National Digital Library.

Spanning 13,988 sq. meters, the fully air-conditioned library accommodates over 210 users, with dedicated reading zones for students, researchers, and faculty. Departmental libraries are also being established across university schools.

🎯 Vision & Mission

Mission: To promote a culture of learning and research among students.

Vision: To adopt innovative digital policies that foster lifelong learning and best management practices.

🛠️ Library Services

  • Reprography: Printing, scanning, and photocopying for all users.
  • Online access to library activities and databases via LAN.
  • Wi-Fi-enabled digital catalog (OPAC) and open resources like NISCARE, NPTEL, e-Gyankosh.
